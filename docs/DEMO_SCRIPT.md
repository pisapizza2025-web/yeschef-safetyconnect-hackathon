# Three-minute demo script

Before going on stage: stop the app, delete `data/db.json`, start it again. Open the Cases page. Pick **Demo Manager** under "Acting as".

| Time | Say | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:25 | "A restaurant's walk-in fridge hits 9°C. The manager needs a decision and a repair person, not a chatbot lecture." | Cases page, point at the four starter cards |
| 0:25 to 1:05 | "Internal first." | Fridge card: **Start at Demo Kitchen A**, **Submit report**. Show Assessment, then Sources: the site procedure and the known contractor, both labelled Internal. Say the contractor is a fictional demo supplier with no number on file |
| 1:05 to 1:40 | "No supplier on file? We say so and hand off honestly." | Back to Cases, start the fridge card at **Demo Kitchen B**. Show the External card: four researched provider cards with their sources, plus two search links (a research snapshot: nobody was contacted, availability is not confirmed). Say: "We never say an engineer is booked." Press one link |
| 1:40 to 2:15 | "A person decides, and the evidence stays." | On the case press **Use** on a suggestion, edit, **Record action**. Switch Acting as to **DEMO Priya, sous chef (fictional)**, add a message. Refresh the page. Point at the Timeline and "Chain verified" |
| 2:15 to 2:40 | "Same engine, other incidents." | Open Cases, start the allergen card (no ingredients invented), the cooking card (add a recheck: the first reading stays), the missing-records card (two rows, two anonymous missing slots) |
| 2:40 to 3:00 | "Find the right answer internally, the right professional externally, and keep the evidence." | Acting as Demo Manager: on the fridge case **Prepare learning card**, **Approve for community**, open **Learning**. Say names and contact details are removed and nothing is shared without approval |

## Do not say

- That Grok is running live (unless the AI variables are set and the call was verified).
- That a contractor was contacted, booked or is available.
- That any provider is available, booked or verified.
- That any food is safe.
- That the suppliers or helpers are real.
